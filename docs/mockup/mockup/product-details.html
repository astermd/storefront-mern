GLP-1 medications naturally regulate appetite to make weight loss achievable. Our microdosing approach refines this by using a conservative, lower-dose strategy designed specifically for modest weight correction.

Instead of rapid escalation, we utilize a slower titration schedule that focuses on quieting “food noise” and promoting earlier fullness. This allows you to achieve your specific aesthetic or health goals with a plan tailored to your body’s sensitivity rather than a standard obesity protocol.

Most patients notice reduced appetite within the first few weeks, with gradual, steady weight loss as your provider adjusts your dose over time.

Semaglutide is FDA-approved and considered safe for most adults. Common side effects are mild and gastrointestinal — your provider will monitor your progress throughout treatment.

Higher doses can lead to greater results, but your provider will titrate your plan based on how your body responds rather than dose alone.
